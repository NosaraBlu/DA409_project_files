Holding Power Accountable Through Data

VA Watchdogs compiles and presents recent crime data from counties and cities across Virginia. Our mission is to promote transparency in policing efforts and empower communities with the information they need to drive meaningful change.

County & City Crime Data

Crime statistics vary dramatically across Virginia's counties and independent cities. VA Watchdogs currently tracks data from seven key jurisdictions: Alexandria, Arlington, Fairfax County, Richmond, Norfolk, Roanoke, and Falls Church. Each faces unique public safety challenges shaped by population density, economic factors, and policing strategies.

By making this data accessible and easy to compare, residents can identify trends, understand where resources are being allocated, and hold local officials accountable for outcomes in their communities.

Policing Efforts & Accountability

Understanding how law enforcement agencies operate is essential to building trust between police departments and the communities they serve. VA Watchdogs tracks key policing metrics including response times, officer-to-resident ratios, use-of-force incidents, and complaint resolution rates across Virginia jurisdictions.

Transparency in policing is not about blame — it is about creating a shared foundation of facts that enables constructive dialogue and evidence-based policy decisions.

Educational Resources

Data without context can mislead. That is why VA Watchdogs provides educational guides that explain how crime data is collected, what the numbers mean, and how citizens can use this information responsibly. Our resources cover topics such as the Uniform Crime Reporting (UCR) program, the National Incident-Based Reporting System (NIBRS), and how to read a department's annual report.

Whether you are a student, journalist, community organizer, or concerned resident, these resources will help you navigate the data with confidence.

About VA Watchdogs

VA Watchdogs was founded with a simple belief: communities that have access to clear, accurate public safety data are better equipped to advocate for themselves. We are a non-partisan, volunteer-driven project dedicated to compiling Virginia crime data into an accessible, user-friendly format.

Our data is sourced from the Virginia State Police, the FBI's Crime Data Explorer, and individual department reports. We do not editorialize — we present the facts and let the community decide what action to take.
