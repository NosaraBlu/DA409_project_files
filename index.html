Why Police Data Matters

Police data is public because it affects everyone.

People should be able to see what is happening in their area without digging through five different PDFs. Virginia agencies report crime and enforcement data through state and federal systems, including the Virginia State Police reporting program and the FBI’s incident-based standards.

This project brings those sources into one place and keeps the focus on comparison across jurisdictions. Northern Virginia looks different from Richmond and Norfolk in density, commuting patterns, tourism, and calls for service. Those differences shape the numbers.

All figures shown here come from official sources: the Virginia State Police, local police department reports, the FBI’s NIBRS system, and the Virginia Open Data Portal.

Five Areas Under the Lens

Not the same Virginia.

These five jurisdictions highlight how different “local context” can look across the Commonwealth. Two are Northern Virginia counties known for lower violent crime rates. Three are independent cities with different urban dynamics and regional pressures.

Data Sources and Methodology

Everything here is traceable.
